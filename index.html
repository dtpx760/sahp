About the SAHP

Serving San Andreas since 1929.

Mission

Who we are

The San Andreas Highway Patrol is the primary law enforcement agency responsible for traffic regulation, highway safety, and criminal interdiction on state highways, freeways, and unincorporated roads across San Andreas.

Founded in 1929 under Governor Clarence Holt, the SAHP was established in response to rapid growth in automobile traffic along the coastal Route 1 corridor. Over the following decades the agency expanded statewide, absorbing the rural Road Patrol and the State Motor Vehicle Inspectorate. Today, SAHP operates from 26 district stations across six troop divisions, employing over 1,800 sworn officers and 400 civilian staff.

Cadet Class 44

About the academy

The San Andreas Highway Patrol is seeking qualified applicants for the 44th Cadet Class. Training begins August 11, 2026 at the SAHP Training Academy, Paleto Bay.

The academy runs 26 weeks and covers firearms qualification, emergency vehicle operations, first aid certification, legal studies, and field training rotations across multiple troop divisions. Graduates are assigned as Trooper I with full state benefits and a competitive salary package.

SAFOIA compliance

Records & transparency

The San Andreas Highway Patrol complies fully with the San Andreas Freedom of Information Act. Records are available through the Office of Public Affairs.

Submit SAFOIA requests in writing to the Office of Public Affairs, SAHP Headquarters, Rockford Hills Government Center, Los Santos, SA 90210. Allow up to 10 business days for processing.

Incident reports

Traffic collision and incident reports available 10 business days after filing. Fee of $0.10 per page.
